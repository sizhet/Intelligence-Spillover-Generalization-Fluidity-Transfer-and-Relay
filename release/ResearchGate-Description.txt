Intelligence Spillover, Generalization, Fluidity, Transfer, and Relay (ISGFTR) is an open, theory-first research project investigating how intelligence capabilities may extend beyond their initially observed tasks, representations, and organizational boundaries. The framework examines five related characteristics—spillover, generalization, fluidity, transfer, and relay—and distinguishes generative, observed, reachable, and evolvable capability boundaries. It explores structural folding and reuse, weak-evidence directional intuition, selective boundary permeability, capability prospecting, system-centric multi-level learning, and recursive structural relay. The v1.0.0 repository comprises ten conceptual research papers, four proposed structural contracts, three illustrative experimental cases, and three exploratory extensions, alongside testable hypotheses and a validation roadmap. These contributions are research proposals rather than claims of demonstrated AGI or experimentally established open-ended intelligence growth. The project invites critical evaluation, counterexamples, reproducible experiments, and collective learning.
