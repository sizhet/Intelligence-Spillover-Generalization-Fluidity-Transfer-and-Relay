## 9. Research principles and claim discipline

1. **Hypothesis before assertion.** Directional intuition and weak evidence may justify a probe, not a validated capability claim.
2. **Separate flow, utility, and growth.** A transferred structure may be unused, harmful, or transient.
3. **Preserve provenance and counterevidence.** Negative results, unresolved residuals, and changed assumptions are reusable research assets.
4. **Distinguish levels of learning.** Improvement in a multi-unit system is not automatically improvement in an individual model's weights.
5. **Avoid AGI overclaiming.** Open-ended exploration, recursive relay, and even durable system improvement do not individually demonstrate AGI.
6. **Support collective learning.** Publish contracts, controls, failure modes, and questions that independent researchers can test or challenge.
