### Research-positioning notes

- The five ISGFTR characteristics are proposed as related but non-equivalent dimensions of intelligence behavior and system design.
- Generative, observed, reachable, and evolvable boundaries are conditional research constructs, not necessarily nested geometric sets.
- Weak evidence may prioritize exploration; it cannot independently justify validated capability claims.
- Mobility, utility, learning, durable growth, recursive self-improvement, and AGI are distinct claims requiring distinct evidence.
- Repeated execution may yield relayable evidence but does not automatically produce learning or growth.
- System-level adaptation with fixed model weights should not be attributed to intrinsic model-weight learning.
- Negative results, uncertainty, counterevidence, costs, and independent replication are integral to the research program.

### Not included in v1.0.0

- A production implementation of the proposed architecture.
- A validated AGI system, demonstrated open-ended intelligence growth, or confirmed recursive self-improvement.
- Completed experiments, measured performance improvements, or established causal effects of the proposed mechanisms.
- A guarantee that any particular folding, permeability, prospecting, or relay mechanism generalizes across domains.

## Versioning and publication policy

- **v1.0.0:** Initial theory-first research corpus, conceptual contracts, illustrative cases, extensions, and research roadmap.
- **Patch releases:** Correct broken links, metadata, typographical errors, and non-substantive clarifications.
- **Minor releases:** Add compatible specifications, new case studies, validation protocols, evidence packages, or extensions.
- **Major releases:** Introduce materially revised definitions, incompatible contract changes, or a substantially reorganized research architecture.

For future evidence-bearing releases, record experimental dates, protocols, datasets or task suites, comparison baselines, negative results, reproducibility artifacts, and the exact scope of supported claims. Do not retroactively present the v1.0.0 conceptual corpus as experimentally validated.
